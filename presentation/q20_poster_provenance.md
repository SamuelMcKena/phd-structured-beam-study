# q=20 A0 poster provenance

This poster deliberately separates **measurement**, **inverse-model inference**, and **numerical correction prediction**.

## Scientific boundary

- The only BeamGage image shown as experimental evidence is the **pre-correction q=20 measurement**.
- There is **no post-correction BeamGage measurement** in the poster.
- The SLM2 correction panels are **model-space numerical predictions** generated by the explicit carrier -> 4F -> fixed +1 iris -> axicon -> propagation route.
- Do not relabel any predicted detector image as a measured corrected beam. The current poster intentionally omits the predicted corrected BeamGage frame altogether.

## SLM2 precompensation source

- Candidate: `q20_topology_regularised_slm2_v2_alpha_0p40`
- Candidate file: `notebooks/experimental/axicon_aberration_correction/candidates/q20_topology_regularised_slm2_candidate.json`
- Source algorithm: compact v2 SLM2 phase correction with scalar strength regularisation.
- Selected strength: `alpha=0.40`.
- Selection rule: strongest tested correction that retained `q=20` on every valid winding contour; larger tested strengths improved intensity closure but changed the inner winding.
- Production validation: `4096 x 4096` numerical grid over a 10 mm physical window.
- Winding contours: `1.0, 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.4, 1.5 mm`, all `q=20`.
- Mean optical prediction: `r=0.8340`, `NRMSE=0.0704`.
- Predicted detector-domain metric: `r=0.9448`, `NRMSE=0.0418`.

The predicted detector-domain metric may be quoted as a metric, but the poster does not show a corrected BeamGage-looking image.

## Hardware status

This is **not yet a hardware-validated correction**. Remaining blockers recorded by the candidate are:

1. independently calibrate SLM2-to-axicon/input-plane conjugacy and coordinate transform;
2. measure parity, rotation, transverse scale, and centre;
3. calibrate the SLM2 1030 nm grey-to-phase LUT / phase stroke;
4. apply the selected mask and acquire a new post-correction q=20 z-stack.
